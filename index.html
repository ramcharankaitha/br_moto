THE SCIENCE BEHIND THE SAFETY

TECHNOLOGY

CARBON FIBER SHELL

Ultra-light 3K carbon composite construction delivers superior impact absorption at a fraction of conventional weight.

MULTI-IMPACT EPS

Dual-density expanded polystyrene liner absorbs and distributes energy across multiple impact zones.

AERO CHANNEL VENTING

12-channel aerodynamic ventilation system channels airflow through the helmet for maximum cooling at any speed.

ANTI-FOG VISOR

Pinlock-ready anti-fog visor with UV400 protection and scratch-resistant coating for crystal-clear vision in all conditions.

DOUBLE D-RING

Race-proven titanium double D-ring retention system ensures the helmet stays securely in place at all speeds.

IMPACT SENSOR READY

Optional integration with smart impact sensors that auto-alert emergency contacts upon crash detection.

OUR
STORY

BR Moto Gear was born on the racetrack. Founded by riders, for riders, we set out to create gear that never compromises — not on protection, not on performance, not on style.

Every helmet we build is tested beyond ECE 22.06 standards. Every piece of riding gear is stress-tested by professional riders before it ever reaches you.

This isn't just gear. It's a commitment. To every rider who trusts us with their safety when it matters most.
